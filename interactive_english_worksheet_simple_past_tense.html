💡 BRIEF GRAMMAR BOX: SIMPLE PAST TENSE

Used to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. Formula: Subject + Verb 2 + Complement

1. Regular Verbs (+ed / +d):
  • Play → Played
  • Visit → Visited
  • Watch → Watched
2. Irregular Verbs (Change Form):
  • Go → Went
  • Eat → Ate | See → Saw
  • Buy → Bought
⏱️ Time Signals: Yesterday, Last night, Last week, Two days ago.
